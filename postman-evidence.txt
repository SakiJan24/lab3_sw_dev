EVIDENCIA CRUD DE ZONAS (curl contra backend en Docker, http://localhost:8090/api)
Nota: en esta maquina el puerto 8080 estaba ocupado por otro proyecto (CVAT),
por eso la prueba se hizo en el puerto 8090; en un entorno limpio corre en 8080/api
tal como pide el enunciado. El contrato (rutas, metodos, codigos, JSON) es identico.
========================================================================

=== 1. CREAR ZONA ===
POST http://localhost:8090/api/zones
Content-Type: application/json
Body:
{"name":"Zona Postman Demo","description":"Zona creada para evidencia de CRUD","capacity":3}

Respuesta:
HTTP/1.1 201 
Vary: Origin
Vary: Access-Control-Request-Method
Vary: Access-Control-Request-Headers
Content-Type: application/json
Transfer-Encoding: chunked
Date: Sun, 27 Sep 2026 04:48:53 GMT

{"id":4,"name":"Zona Postman Demo","description":"Zona creada para evidencia de CRUD","capacity":3,"creatureCount":0}

------------------------------------------------------------------------
=== 2. LISTAR ZONAS ===
GET http://localhost:8090/api/zones

Respuesta:
HTTP/1.1 200 
Vary: Origin
Vary: Access-Control-Request-Method
Vary: Access-Control-Request-Headers
Content-Type: application/json
Transfer-Encoding: chunked
Date: Sun, 27 Sep 2026 04:48:57 GMT

[{"id":1,"name":"Zona N1","description":"Zona de prueba 1 para evidencia N+1","capacity":5,"creatureCount":1},{"id":2,"name":"Zona N2","description":"Zona de prueba 2 para evidencia N+1","capacity":5,"creatureCount":1},{"id":3,"name":"Zona N3","description":"Zona de prueba 3 para evidencia N+1","capacity":5,"creatureCount":1},{"id":4,"name":"Zona Postman Demo","description":"Zona creada para evidencia de CRUD","capacity":3,"creatureCount":0}]

------------------------------------------------------------------------
